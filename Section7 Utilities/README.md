## 📌 Overview

This section is an extensive hands-on reference containing **58 utility topics** in Bootstrap 5. It covers background colors, opacity, borders, display modes, flexbox mastery, responsive floats, link styling, object-fit, overflow, positioning, sizing, shadows, typography, and z-index.

## 📖 Utility Categories & Key Classes

### 1. Backgrounds & Borders (`01`–`10`)
- **Backgrounds**: `.bg-primary`, `.bg-secondary`, `.bg-gradient`, `.bg-opacity-25`, `.bg-opacity-50`, `.bg-opacity-75`.
- **Borders**: `.border`, `.border-top-0`, `.border-primary`, `.border-opacity-50`, `.border-1` through `.border-5`.
- **Border Radius**: `.rounded`, `.rounded-top`, `.rounded-circle`, `.rounded-pill`, `.rounded-0` to `.rounded-5`.

### 2. Text, Colors & Display (`11`–`15`)
- **Text & Opacity**: `.text-primary`, `.text-muted`, `.text-opacity-50`, `.text-opacity-75`.
- **Display Modes**: `.d-none`, `.d-inline`, `.d-inline-block`, `.d-block`, `.d-grid`, `.d-table`, `.d-print-none`, `.d-print-inline`.

### 3. Flexbox Suite (`16`–`28`)
- **Direction**: `.flex-row`, `.flex-row-reverse`, `.flex-column`, `.flex-column-reverse`.
- **Justify Content**: `.justify-content-start`, `.justify-content-end`, `.justify-content-center`, `.justify-content-between`, `.justify-content-around`, `.justify-content-evenly`.
- **Align Items & Self**: `.align-items-start`, `.align-items-center`, `.align-items-end`, `.align-self-*`.
- **Fill, Grow & Shrink**: `.flex-fill`, `.flex-grow-1`, `.flex-shrink-1`.
- **Auto Margins**: `.ms-auto`, `.me-auto`, `.mt-auto`, `.mb-auto`.
- **Wrap & Order**: `.flex-nowrap`, `.flex-wrap`, `.order-1` to `.order-5`.
- **Media Object Pattern**: Modern flexbox-based media object recreation.

### 4. Floats, Interactions & Links (`29`–`33`)
- **Floats**: `.float-start`, `.float-end`, `.float-none`, responsive `.float-md-start`.
- **Interactions**: `.user-select-all`, `.user-select-auto`, `.user-select-none`, `.pe-none`, `.pe-auto`.
- **Links**: `.link-opacity-50`, `.link-underline-primary`, `.link-offset-2`.

### 5. Object Fit, Opacity & Overflow (`34`–`37`)
- **Object Fit**: `.object-fit-contain`, `.object-fit-cover`, `.object-fit-fill`, `.object-fit-scale`.
- **Opacity**: `.opacity-0`, `.opacity-25`, `.opacity-50`, `.opacity-75`, `.opacity-100`.
- **Overflow**: `.overflow-auto`, `.overflow-hidden`, `.overflow-visible`, `.overflow-scroll`.

### 6. Positioning & Alignment (`38`–`42`, `56`, `58`)
- **Positions**: `.position-relative`, `.position-absolute`, `.position-fixed`, `.position-sticky`.
- **Offsets & Centering**: `.top-0`, `.top-50`, `.start-50`, `.translate-middle`, `.translate-middle-x`, `.translate-middle-y`.
- **Z-Index**: `.z-0`, `.z-1`, `.z-2`, `.z-3`, `.z-n1`.
- **Vertical Alignment**: `.align-baseline`, `.align-top`, `.align-middle`, `.align-bottom`, `.align-text-top`.

### 7. Sizing, Spacing & Shadows (`43`–`45`)
- **Shadows**: `.shadow-none`, `.shadow-sm`, `.shadow`, `.shadow-lg`.
- **Sizing**: `.w-25`, `.w-50`, `.w-75`, `.w-100`, `.w-auto`, `.h-25`, `.h-50`, `.h-100`, `.mw-100`, `.mh-100`, `.min-vh-100`.
- **Gaps**: `.gap-0` through `.gap-5` across flex and grid containers.

### 8. Typography Utilities (`46`–`55`, `57`)
- **Alignment & Transform**: `.text-start`, `.text-center`, `.text-end`, `.text-lowercase`, `.text-uppercase`, `.text-capitalize`.
- **Wrap & Break**: `.text-wrap`, `.text-nowrap`, `.text-break`.
- **Fonts & Decoration**: `.fs-1` to `.fs-6`, `.fw-bold`, `.fw-normal`, `.fst-italic`, `.lh-1`, `.lh-sm`, `.lh-base`, `.lh-lg`, `.font-monospace`, `.text-decoration-underline`, `.text-decoration-none`.
- **Visibility**: `.visible`, `.invisible`.
